## How this theory came about

RFT did not emerge in a library. It matured for thirty years as an inner picture, before — starting in late 2024 and with the help of several AI systems — it was finally formalised.

### 1992–2002: A long, broad path through university

Ten years of study — not for lack of capability, but through an unusual interlocking of three constraints that turned out, in hindsight, to be productive:

**Economic situation and dual workload.** The post-reunification years in Germany were economically difficult; the worry of finding no job after graduation made every extension of the studies rational. In parallel I worked as a student assistant at the university **and** with a 30-hour contract at **Orga Kartensysteme in Paderborn** — smartcard technology, signal processing. This double load left little time for studying, but it produced the engineering-near grounding that later made the difference.

**The examination paradox.** With only three to seven new students per cohort, often only one of two lectures on a topic was offered — one counted as exam admission, the other delivered the exam content. The constraint of having to take content in just one semester (and waiting for it in others) extended the studies structurally.

**The hidden gain — broad view instead of tunnel vision.** Precisely this "scattering effect" later decided the shape of RFT: because I had *brushed against* many fields without sinking into one, I could see cross-connections that specialised theorists could not. The RFT thesis — that Maxwell, Schrödinger, Newton, and General Relativity drop out as special-case solutions of one equation — requires exactly that cross-vision.

The *engineering perspective* (signal processing at Orga Kartensysteme + student-assistant work) is directly recognisable in RFT: space as a vibration-capable medium, mass as impedance, gravity as a stress gradient — these are **electrical-engineering categories**, not differential-geometry categories.

### 1993: Three Discomforts and the Urbox Hypothesis

In this long, broad period of study, three aspects of modern physics ran into engineering-like resistance:

1. **String theory and its rolled-up dimensions.** Why should particles be individual, isolated strings in Calabi-Yau manifolds, instead of — like electrons in a metal block — excitations distributed across a continuous medium?

2. **QM exchange particles.** Virtual particles that carry more energy than the final result, solely to keep the calculation consistent — a "mathematical fetish".

3. **Philosophy has failed.** It has been unable to explain the world since Newton. There must be a more graphic path with a different perspective.

From these three resistances, a single question crystallised:

> **"How would I build a universe?"**

The answer — thought as an engineer, not as a mathematician:

1. **Begin with a matrix.**
2. **Time must not be an independent dimension** (it would violate causality).
3. **Particles are resonances of a universal field**, not isolated points.

The inner metaphor that took hold:

> *"Sliding-contact sparks on space."*

A sliding contact is not a point-like junction but a *travelling touch between two media* (pantograph and overhead wire). The spark is the event that springs from the contact — not an object, but a manifestation of the contact. Translated into later RFT terminology:

- Space = the carrying medium → **space matrix**
- Sliding contact = the coupling of the vortex to space → **anchor point (AP)**
- Spark = the observable particle → **resonance mode**

The name "Urbox" for this core notion came only in 2025, retrospectively. What existed for thirty years was the picture, not the name.

### September 2024: The Starting Shot

In late summer 2024 I went through a psychosomatic rehabilitation, which ended on **17 September 2024** — my birthday. The following weekend, I opened a ChatGPT chat for the first time, to finally formalise the thirty-year-old idea.

The concrete trigger was not the string critique or the philosophy dissatisfaction, but a specific paradox in established cosmology: **dark energy and dark matter as circular constructs**, plus black holes as singularities of varying size. RFT resolves both: dark energy as a "bow-wave effect" (gravitational effect without mass), black holes as mode jumps in the resonance field.

### 2025–2026: Formalisation with AI Assistance

What followed was a fifteen-month dialogue with several AI systems — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek, locally Qwen — which produced not only the theory but also the *tool* for the theory. Working protocols, a Domain Center, handover letters, a local RAG system on dedicated hardware. The theory-process and the tool-process are intertwined — both support each other.

The master equation stood structurally stable from **19 January 2025**, when in twelve hours five consolidating documents emerged. It is the constant in everything that came after.
